## Collect
Almighty God, whose never-failing providence governs all things in heaven and earth: we humbly ask you to put away from us all hurtful things, and give us whatever may be profitable for us through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |From Genesis to Revelation, You have revealed Yourself as the LORD—a God merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness. Throughout all of salvation history, Your heart has moved with profound compassion toward a fallen world, pursuing us in our weakness and redeeming us through the grace of Jesus Christ. <br><br> As we reflect on Your Word this week, open our eyes to behold the depth of Your character. Forgive us for the times we have been merely hearers of Your Word and not doers. Help us to examine our lives in the mirror of Your truth, casting off indifference, coldness, and partiality. <br><br> Teach us, Lord, to walk in the way You have always operated. Just as You have chosen those who are poor in the eyes of the world to be rich in faith and heirs of Your kingdom, give us hearts that burn with genuine love for the vulnerable, the needy, and the suffering. <br><br> Transform our lives and our church to be an active, living reflection of Your mercy. May our faith be pure and undefiled as we extend Your hands of compassion, continuing Your great story of redemption in our communities and across the world today. <br><br> In the gracious and holy name of Jesus we pray. <br><br> **Amen.**|
