## Collect
Almighty God, whose never-failing providence governs all things in heaven and earth: we humbly ask you to put away from us all hurtful things, and give us whatever may be profitable for us through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father, <br><br> Thank you for your incredible grace. We pray that we would not receive this grace in vain, knowing that today is the day of salvation, and right now is the time of your favour. Forgive us for the times we have treated following Jesus merely as a way to upgrade our existing lives, rather than taking up our cross to follow Him. <br><br> Help us to live lives worthy of the Gospel of Christ. When we face trouble, hardship, and distress—when our work, our relationships, or our ministry feel heavy and weary—grant us the strength to endure. Fill us with your Spirit so that we may respond not with cynicism or self-protection, but with sincere love, patience, kindness, and integrity. Keep our hearts warm toward you and toward one another, especially when the path is costly.<br><br> Open our eyes to see reality through the eyes of faith. Teach us to count the cost and know that Jesus is worthy. When we are tempted to seek comfort, approval, or security in this world, anchor our hearts in the truth that we are already fully known, deeply loved, and endowed with infinite wealth in Christ.<br><br> We praise you for Jesus—that though we may have nothing in the eyes of the world, in Him we possess everything. We praise you that we are made truly alive with a joy that cannot be stolen and a glory that will never fade. Compel our hearts by your love, and keep us steadfast as we serve you together as your church. <br><br> In Jesus’ name we pray, **Amen.**|
