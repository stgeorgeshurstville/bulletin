## Collect
Let your merciful ears, Lord God, be open to the prayers of your people; and so that they may obtain their petitions, make them to ask such things as will please you; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

## Week A

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father,<br><br>Thank You that You do not leave us to be tossed about like windchimes by every changing breeze of our culture. Forgive us for the subtle ways we allow our worth, security, or identity to be shaped by academic achievement, career status, relationships, or comfortable living rather than by You.<br><br>Thank You for Your incredible promises—that You live in us, walk with us, and call us Your beloved sons and daughters. Help us to rest so deeply in our identity in Christ that the pull to compromise or yoke ourselves to worldly idols loses its power.<br><br>Lord, grant us the grace and wisdom to stand faithfully in our daily lives while remaining whole-heartedly set apart for You. Give us pure motives, clean hands, and the courage to walk in honest community with other believers. May our lives bring You joy and glorify Your name.<br><br>In Jesus' name,<br><br>**Amen**.|
