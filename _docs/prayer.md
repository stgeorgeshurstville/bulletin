## Collect
Almighty and eternal God, grant that we may grow in faith, hope, and love; and that we may obtain what you promise, make us love what you command; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **Church Ministries** |**Evangelism teams** <br><br> Gracious Lord, we thank You for entrusting us with the mission of sharing the Gospel and for continually opening doors for our church to connect with our community and meet new people. Give us hearts that love others and eyes that are attentive to the people You place around us. Help us to bear witness to Christ with courage and sincerity in our daily lives, bringing the hope of the Gospel to those we meet.<br><br> Lord, we especially thank You for the two days of outreach at the recent CanCare Expo. Thank You for the opportunities to connect with many people in the community through our stall activities, Gospel tracts, and information about our church, and for the conversations and sharing that took place. We pray that You would use every Gospel seed that was sown and continue to work in people's hearts, stirring in them a desire to know more about the Gospel and, in Your perfect timing, to come to know Jesus Christ more deeply.<br><br> Please continue to strengthen and unite our evangelism team as we serve together in outreach, invitations, and follow-up ministries. Give us wisdom not only to sow the seeds of the Gospel, but also to patiently build relationships, care for, and walk alongside those we meet. May every encounter become an opportunity for You to work in their lives.<br><br> Continue to use our church as a faithful witness for You in our community. Help us not to rely on our own methods or abilities, but on the power of the Holy Spirit as we faithfully proclaim Christ. May more people come to know You through us, experience Your love, and receive the new life and hope that are found in Christ. Amen.|

May everything we do reflect the love and grace of Christ.<br><br> May our Cantonese congregation truly become a community that knows You and makes You known. Give us generous hearts to love one another and to reach out to those around us, so that through our lives and our fellowship, more people may see Christ, come to know Your grace, and receive the hope and new life of the Gospel.
