## Collect
Lord, we pray that your grace may always uphold and encourage us, and make us continually to be given to all good works; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father, <br><br> All true honour, wisdom, and strength belong to You alone. Jesus has shown us what true glory, power, and wisdom are. <br><br> Forgive us for how much we love to show off. We're sorry for our pride. We brag about our marks, our jobs, our achievements, and our efforts just to look impressive. <br><br> We know that no amount of bragging will earn people's true love and approval. We are so easily impressed by things that aren't truly impressive, and we exhaust ourselves trying to get noticed by people who don't really care about us. <br><br> Forgive us, and protect us from our own foolishness. Thank You for the Lord Jesus, who has shown us true wisdom, power, and glory. Thank You that we don't have to earn Your love and approval, nor do we have to maintain Your love through our achievements. Please teach us Your wisdom.<br><br> In Jesus' name we pray, **Amen.**|

| **Special Items & Events** | **Prayer of Generosity** <br><br> Heavenly Father,<br><br> Father of mercies and God of all comfort, <br><br> We come before You with hearts full of thanksgiving. We praise You that though our Lord Jesus Christ was rich, yet for our sake He became poor, so that through His poverty we might become rich. You see the unexpected trials and costs our fellowship has weathered, and You see the shortfall in our budget. Yet we know that when we are weak, You are strong, and Your grace is sufficient for us. You are the God who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food. We bring this financial need honestly to Your feet, asking that You provide all that is required for the Gospel work to flourish. <br><br> We pray especially for our leaders and decision-makers. Grant them godly wisdom, vigilance, and sound judgment as they steward the common resources entrusted to this church. Give them clarity in prioritising the ministry of the Gospel, courage to make prudent decisions, and the grace to manage these gifts with complete transparency and integrity—taking care to do what is right not only in Your eyes, but also in the sight of all people. <br><br> Awaken all our hearts to see our giving as an act of joyful discipleship. Forgive us where we have held back out of hesitation or fear. Let us give not reluctantly or under compulsion, but as those compelled by the love of Christ.
