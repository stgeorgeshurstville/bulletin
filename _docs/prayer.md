## Collect
Lord, give your people grace to withstand the temptations of the world, the flesh, and the devil, and with pure hearts and minds to follow you the only God; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father, <br><br> We have foolishly relied on ourselves—our strength, our cleverness. We have grown proud. <br><br> Thank you that you have planned for us to become like Jesus, from even before the beginning of the world. <br><br> Thank you that you have said ‘no’ to us. <br><br> Help us to trust that even your ‘no’ is good for us. Thank you that your grace is sufficient for us, and that you will finish the work you started in us. <br><br> Help us to boast in Jesus, and to bear witness to your power in him. <br><br> Help us to be a ‘weak’ church. <br><br> In his mighty and precious name we pray, <br><br> **Amen.**|

| **Mission Partners** | **Compassion** <br><br> *General info: Compassion is a Christian international holistic child development organisation. In response to God’s call, Compassion gives hope to the hopeless, spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ in a culturally relevant, non-coercive way. Through our Child Sponsorship Program, more than 2 million children are currently being released from poverty in Jesus’ name. <br><br> **A Prayer for Compassion** <br><br> Heavenly Father,<br><br> We come before You with hearts full of gratitude for Your boundless love and mercy. You are the defender of the vulnerable and the provider for those in need. Today, we lift up the global ministry of **Compassion International.** <br><br> **For Holistic Growth and Development** <br><br> Lord, we thank You that Compassion is rooted firmly as a Christian, international, holistic child development organization. We pray that You bless this whole-child approach. As they work to release children from poverty, look after every aspect of these young lives: <br><br> - Spiritually: May they grow to know the deep, transformative love of Jesus.
- Physically: Provide them with nourishing food, clean water, and safe shelter.
- Mona and Emotionally: Surround them with safe communities, mentors, and the emotional support needed to heal and thrive.
- Educationally: Open doors for learning, giving them the practical tools and skills to break the cycle of poverty.
 <br><br> **For the Children** <br><br> We pray for the millions of children currently in Compassion’s care. Protect them, comfort them, and remind them of their immense value in Your eyes. Where poverty has whispered lies of hopelessness, let Your truth declare that they are chosen, loved, and filled with divine purpose. <br><br> **For the Ministry and Staff** <br><br> We ask for Your continued wisdom and favor upon Compassion's leadership, local church partners, and field staff. Grant them discerning hearts to navigate diverse cultures with grace and humility. Keep them safe, renew their energy, and give them the cultural relevance to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ in a gentle, non-coercive way that honors the dignity of every community they serve. <br><br> **For the Sponsors** <br><br> Bless the sponsors whose generosity and prayers bridge the gap across oceans and borders. Multiply their gifts, and weave a deep bond of encouragement between every sponsor and child. <br><br> "And Jesus grew in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and man." — Luke 2:52 <br><br> May Compassion continue to be a beacon of hope, faithfully releasing children from poverty in Jesus' name, nurturing them into the full potential You created for them. <br><br> **Amen.** |
| **Church Ministries** |**Discipleship groups** <br><br> Heavenly Father, <br><br> We thank you so much for our Discipleship groups and for the gift of meeting together. Thank you for providing these spaces this year as places of growth, where we could dive deep into the truths of 2 Corinthians and learn directly from your Word. <br><br> Lord, we ask that you continue to open our hearts to the transformation you desire for us. Teach us to respond fully to your calling in every area of our lives—especially in our generosity. Give us grace to give freely, love sacrificially, and live as humble followers and leaders who model Christ, rejecting pride and boastfulness.<br><br> We pray that our Discipleship groups will always be a haven for those seeking genuine rest, hope, and community. May anyone who enters these spaces find a warm, welcoming family willing to love, support, and bear standard burdens together. <br><br> In Jesus' name we pray, **Amen.**|

| **Special Items & Events** | **Prayer of Generosity** <br><br> Heavenly Father,<br><br> Father of mercies and God of all comfort, <br><br> We come before You with hearts full of thanksgiving. We praise You that though our Lord Jesus Christ was rich, yet for our sake He became poor, so that through His poverty we might become rich. Thank You for Your surpassing grace, and for entrusting us with the ministry of reconciliation in our church and community. <br><br> Lord, You know our current circumstances. You see the unexpected trials and costs our fellowship has weathered, and You see the shortfall in our budget. Yet we know that when we are weak, You are strong, and Your grace is sufficient for us. You are the God who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food. We bring this financial need honestly to Your feet, asking that You provide all that is required for the Gospel work to flourish. <br><br> We pray especially for our leaders and decision-makers. Grant them godly wisdom, vigilance, and sound judgment as they steward the common resources entrusted to this church. Give them clarity in prioritising the ministry of the Gospel, courage to make prudent decisions, and the grace to manage these gifts with complete transparency and integrity—taking care to do what is right not only in Your eyes, but also in the sight of all people. <br><br> Awaken all our hearts to see our giving as an act of joyful discipleship. Forgive us where we have held back out of hesitation or fear. Let us give not reluctantly or under compulsion, but as those compelled by the love of Christ. May our sacrificial generosity meet every need and overflow in many thanksgivings to You. <br><br> We entrust our leaders, our resources, and our future into Your faithful hands. <br><br> In Jesus' name we pray, **Amen.**|
