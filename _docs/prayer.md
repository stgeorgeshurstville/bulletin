## Collect
Lord God, you declare your almighty power chiefly in showing mercy and pity: grant us such a measure of your grace that, running in the way of your commandments, we may obtain your promises, and share in heavenly treasure; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Gracious Heavenly Father,<br><br>We confess today that our material security is often the final frontier we hesitate to surrender. It is easy to look like the "complete package" on the outside while harboring a self-reliant pride that grips tightly to what we have. Forgive us for the times a scarcity mindset drives us to fear and self-preservation. <br><br> We thank You for the ultimate act of grace: our Lord Jesus Christ, who though He was rich, for our sakes became poor, so that we might become rich. <br><br> By Your Spirit, transform us into a people who G-I-V-E: <br><br> - Genuinely loving others through tangible sacrifice, not just out of duty. <br><br> - Invited into Christ’s abundance, finding our ultimate security in Him. <br><br> - Vowing to follow through on our commitments to Your kingdom with integrity. <br><br> - Equalizing the needs of Your church by cheerfully sharing our surplus <br><br> Lord, we face a crushing cost of living, but we pray that the brokenness and greed of this world would never dictate the temperature of our generosity. When the world squeezes its fists in fear, may we open our hands in faith, trusting You for our daily manna. <br><br> Convert our hearts and our wallets, so that Your grace may overflow through us. <br><br> In Jesus’ name we pray,<br>Amen|
