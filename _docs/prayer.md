## Collect
Stir up, Lord, the wills of your faithful people, that they may produce abundantly the fruit of good works, and receive your abundant reward; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father,<br><br> We confess that our hearts are perpetual factories of idols. Even with good things—our family, our reputation, our comfort, and even how we do church and ministry—we so easily turn your gifts into things we worship and cling to. Open our eyes to see the idols we have built. Make us slow to anger when our preferences or pride are threatened, and quick to confess and repent.<br><br> Thank you that you are not confined to a building made by human hands, and that we do not have to guess where to find you. Thank you that Jesus is where you have chosen to be found, and that in him we can meet you face to face. We praise you that by your Spirit, you are extending your presence and your lordship everywhere that the name of Jesus is preached.<br><br> Fill us with your Holy Spirit, just as you filled Stephen. Help us to see Christ in his glory clearly and without hindrance. Take away our fear and half-heartedness, and give us the courage to speak truth and live boldly—pointing to Jesus and bearing witness to him in all we do. Pour out your supernatural compassion into our hearts, enabling us to love and forgive even those who wrong us or do us harm. We know we can only live this way by the power of your Spirit.<br><br> Do this in us, Lord, so that all glory may go to Christ alone.<br><br> In Jesus’ name we pray, Amen.|
