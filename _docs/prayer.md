## Collect
Lord, give your people grace to withstand the temptations of the world, the flesh, and the devil, and with pure hearts and minds to follow you the only God; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father, <br><br> We have foolishly relied on ourselves—our strength, our cleverness. We have grown proud. <br><br> Thank you that you have planned for us to become like Jesus, from even before the beginning of the world. <br><br> Thank you that you have said ‘no’ to us. <br><br> Help us to trust that even your ‘no’ is good for us.
