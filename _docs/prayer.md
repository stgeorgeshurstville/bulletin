## Collect
Let your merciful ears, Lord God, be open to the prayers of your people; and so that they may obtain their petitions, make them to ask such things as will please you; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father,<br><br>Thank You that You do not leave us to be tossed about like windchimes by every changing breeze of our culture. Forgive us for the subtle ways we allow our worth, security, or identity to be shaped by academic achievement, career status, relationships, or comfortable living rather than by You.<br><br>Thank You for Your incredible promises—that You live in us, walk with us, and call us Your beloved sons and daughters. Help us to rest so deeply in our identity in Christ that the pull to compromise or yoke ourselves to worldly idols loses its power.<br><br>Lord, grant us the grace and wisdom to stand faithfully in our daily lives while remaining whole-heartedly set apart for You. Give us pure motives, clean hands, and the courage to walk in honest community with other believers. May our lives bring You joy and glorify Your name.<br><br>In Jesus' name,<br><br>**Amen**.|

Lil mentors colleagues in Eurasia to remain dependent on God’s Word and conducts trauma healing workshops for children._ <br><br>Thanksgiving & Prayer for August 2026<br>Praise God for the opportunity Lil has to join another group’s yearly conference in Asia (26-29th August). Ask that God would guide Lil as she prepares to lead a devotional and a prayer session during the conference. The theme of this group’s conference is “God's faithfulness”. One translator wrote, “One of the most significant insights I gained was that a translator is not simply transferring words but is deeply committed to conveying the message intended by the biblical author.” Ask that this translator and others in the Common Language team would put this new insight into practice as they continue to translate.<br><br>Ask that the Greek Interlinear team Phil works with would be faithful and diligent in their checking and revising so that the New Testament would be ready for the consultant check in September.<br><br>Praise God for the encouraging times that we had as we’ve visited and shared at our partner churches and at small groups during the first half of this year.
