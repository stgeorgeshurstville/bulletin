## Collect
Merciful God, it is by your gift alone that your faithful people offer you true and acceptable service; grant that we may so faithfully serve you in this life that we fail not finally to obtain your heavenly promises; through the merits of Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father,<br><br>We thank you that you are a good and overwhelmingly generous God. In a world that constantly tells us that "greed is good" and drives us to tightly grasp our time, money, and security, we thank you for liberating us through the Gospel. Thank you that while we were still trapped in our selfishness, Christ died for us. You have fundamentally changed our lens from "greed is good" to **God is good.** <br><br> Lord, we confess that faithful stewardship is often hard. We confess the moments we are tempted by the things of this world or tempted to treat our resources as our own rather than yours <br><br> We pray that you would make us a community like Titus—earnest, zealous, and deeply committed to encouraging and building one another up. Help us to hold each other accountable, to walk together when the road is difficult, and to find true joy in open-handed generosity. <br><br> We ask that you protect our church from poor stewardship that would ever distract or turn people away from you. Instead, Father, use our wallets, our time, and our resources as a universal language that points the outside world directly to the Gospel. May the way we selflessly share and sacrifice cause those around us to see a beautiful first picture of your grace. <br><br> Remind us daily that we own nothing. We are simply your stewards. Guard us against the pride of playing the king, and keep us faithfully, joyfully managing your resources until King Jesus returns. <br><br> In His mighty and beautiful name we pray, <br><br> Amen|
| **Mission & Vision** | **A prayer for Nepal** <br><br> Context: Early yesterday morning (26 Aug), a flash flood tore through the Rasuwa region of northern Nepal (190 KM from Kathmandu), sweeping through the towns of Timure and Syabrubesi before surging downstream through the Trishuli valley. <br><br> Around two hundred people have died, many are injured, hundreds are missing (including more than 600 hundred tourists), and whole communities have lost the roads, bridges and homes that connected them to safety. Families further downstream are still watching the water, waiting to see what comes next. This is devastated and no one expected it could ever happen. <br><br> Nepal governments are trying to help affected communities in this difficult time and lots of security forces in the field finding dead bodies and searching for missing's.<br><br> - **We pray for immediate relief and provision:** Lord, where roads, bridges, and homes have been entirely washed away, we ask that you make a way for emergency aid to break through. We pray that those who have been displaced will quickly find safe, warm shelters, and that those who are injured will receive prompt, life-saving medical treatment. <br> - **We pray for the missing and disconnected:** Father, with hundreds of locals and over 600 tourists currently missing or out of contact, we pray for their safety. In the midst of broken infrastructure and downed communications, please open up channels for them to connect with their anxious families. Guide the security forces, rescue teams, and local authorities as they painstakingly search the fields and waters; grant them endurance, wisdom, and success.<br> - **We pray for the grieving:** Our hearts break for the families who have lost their loved ones to this tragedy, and those whose entire lives and homes have been swept away. In their immense shock and pain, we ask for your direct, supernatural comfort. Be a refuge for them when everything else has collapsed. <br> - **We pray for the local churches:** Lord, we ask that you empower the local churches in Nepal during this hour of darkness. Fill them with your Holy Spirit so that they may be the hands and feet of Jesus. Give them the resources, the strength, and the profound love needed to serve, shelter, and care for their suffering neighbors. May your light shine brightly through their open-handed generosity. |
