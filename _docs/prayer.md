## Collect
Keep your Church, Lord God, with your continual mercy, and because the frailty of man without you cannot but fall, keep us always under your protection, and lead us to everything that makes for our salvation; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

| **From the Passage** |Heavenly Father,<br><br> We come before You today acknowledging how easily our eyes are drawn to the surface of things. We confess that we live in a world obsessed with optics, personal platforms, and human applause. Too often, Lord, we allow those same worldly values to seep into our own hearts, our relationships, and our ministries.<br><br> Forgive us for the times we have fought for our own egos rather than humbly standing for Your truth. Forgive us when we have compared ourselves horizontally with those around us—measuring our worth by others' flaws or seeking significance in human approval. Forgive us for any moments we have used our words or influence to tear others down, protect an image, or manufacture an impression that isn't true to who we are in secret.<br><br> Father, help us to look to Jesus, who embodied true meekness and spiritual power.<br><br> - **Give us humble courage in conflict.** Teach us to lay down worldly weapons of intimidation, sharp rhetoric, and self-defense. Help us take every thought captive to the obedience of Christ, fighting gently yet boldly for the hearts of others and the truth of Your Gospel.<br> - **Make us people of consistent integrity.** May who we are in public be exactly who we are in private. Grant us the grace to use whatever influence or authority You have entrusted to us—in our families, workplaces, and church—not to build ourselves up, but to consistently build others up in faith and love. <br> - **Anchor our significance in You alone.** Remind us that our worth is not found in our achievements, our wisdom, or our reputation, but in the finished work of Jesus on the cross. Teach us to reject self-promotion and horizontal comparison. May our only boast be that we know and understand You—the God of steadfast love, justice, and righteousness.<br><br> As we go out into this week, deliver us from the trap of performative optics and make us a people of genuine spiritual substance. Deliver us from seeking the temporary praise of men, and fix our hearts on the only evaluation that ultimately matters: Your well-done.<br><br> We pray all these things in the strong, humble, and exalted name of Jesus.<br><br> **Amen.**|
